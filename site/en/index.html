How it works

Three steps and you're filming.

Tap "Start recording"

Pick the camera, quality and timer. Recording is always started by you.

Switch to any app

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or notes with your script — Hahacam keeps recording in the background.

Tap "Stop"

In the notification or in the app. The video lands in your gallery, folder Movies/Hahacam.

FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Does Hahacam record with the screen locked?

Yes, once recording has started. If it stops after locking on your phone, turn off battery optimization for Hahacam (Settings → Apps → Hahacam → Battery → "Unrestricted").

Why is there a green dot at the top?

It's Android's system indicator: it appears whenever any app uses the camera. It can't and shouldn't be removed. It doesn't appear in the video.

Recording stopped when I opened the camera in Instagram

Android gives the camera to the app that's on screen. Hahacam saves everything recorded up to that point — just start a new recording after your story.

How do I also record the screen for a reaction?

Turn on Android's built-in screen recorder together with Hahacam, then combine the two videos in CapCut or another editor. Tip: turn off the microphone in the screen recorder so the two apps don't share it.

Where are videos saved and how much space do they take?

In your gallery, folder Movies/Hahacam. 1080p is about 1 GB per 15 minutes, 720p about half that.
